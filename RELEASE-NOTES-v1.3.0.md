# Version 1.3.0 - November 2026

Seth Douglas and Nidhal Mghirbi. Version 1.3.0, November 2026. A preprint; it has not been peer reviewed.

This version adds a Lean 4 core, which proves the printed profile bounds of Theorem 1.1 (with upper constant 7000), Theorems 3.1–3.3 and other printed results, the profile-only part of Corollary 1.2 and, conditionally on the explicit premise `PermSectorBound`, the H₃ finitary-three-cycle-kernel conclusion of Corollary 1.3; lean/README.md and the new Appendix C, "Lean verification", give the exact scope.

The theorems of version 1.2.0 are unchanged, as are their printed numbers and the abstract. The earlier Lean 4.30.0 certificate package is retained. Licensing extends to the core under the existing CC BY 4.0/MIT terms. Two bibliography spacing warnings are removed without changing their words.

The all-versions DOI remains 10.5281/zenodo.23027624. The version 1.3.0 version DOI will be recorded after minting.
